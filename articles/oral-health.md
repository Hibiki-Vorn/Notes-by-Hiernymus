Singapore faces rising youth health issues including obesity and myopia.  
新加坡面临日益严重的青年健康问题，包括肥胖和近视。  

1 in 3 Singaporean teens suffers from myopia due to excessive screen time.  
因过度使用屏幕，1/3新加坡青少年患有近视。  

Vaping among teenagers persists despite national bans.  
尽管全国禁止，青少年吸电子烟现象持续存在。  

Elderly health challenges involve chronic diseases and isolation.  
老年健康挑战包括慢性疾病和孤独问题。  

40% of seniors over 65 manage diabetes or hypertension.  
40%的65岁以上老年人需控制糖尿病或高血压。  

Poorly designed HDB flats increase fall risks for the elderly.  
设计不良的组屋增加了老年人跌倒风险。

Mental health stigma prevents elderly from seeking therapy.  
心理健康污名使老年人不愿寻求治疗。  

Health inequality manifests as delayed treatment in low-income groups.  
健康不平等表现为低收入群体延迟就医。  

The Healthier SG initiative shifts focus to preventive care.  
"健康SG"计划将重心转向预防性医疗。  

Active Ageing Centres combat isolation through community activities.  
乐龄活动中心通过社区活动对抗孤立。  

Digital healthcare tools must balance convenience and human connection.  
数字医疗工具需平衡便捷性与人文关怀。  

Health literacy is becoming Singapore's new core competency.  
健康素养正成为新加坡新核心竞争力。
